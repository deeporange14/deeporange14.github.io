About the dataset

Telemetry supports the modeling, validation, and optimization of complex vehicle systems.
The published collection includes vehicle-state information recorded through three acquisition methods

  • ROS bags
  • CAN logs
  • External DAQ sensor data

All data is synchronized, timestamped and packaged into a MATLAB-friendly format (.mat) to facilitate comprehensive analysis with minimal preprocessing.
